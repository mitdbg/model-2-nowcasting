Groupings

Groupings contain entities. Entities are the economic objects whose hidden states the model tracks; groupings are what many observations are about: a company's filing covers the entities it consolidates, a satellite reading covers the entities at a site, and a Census count covers a cell of sector, size class and region. The model predicts an observation about a grouping from the grouping's members on the observation's date, each at its weight. Membership changes over time, for example when a company sells a plant.
