Evidence: each observation against the model

Dots are reported values. The line is the model's prediction of the reported quantity, and the band adds the observation's own noise: about 90% of dots should fall inside it (red dots fall outside). Company-level reports cover several operations, so they predict the sum over the report scope, not this operation alone.

Fitted parameters

Each free parameter of this operation: the prior's 90% range (grey), the fit's 90% range (outlined) and point estimate, and the truth (orange tick). "sd ratio" is the fit's sd over the prior's: near 1 means the data taught little. Offsets such as õ and α are measured from window constants (D̄, x̄, log K₀) that the fit sets from its own window, so fit and truth can differ by a convention as well as by error.
