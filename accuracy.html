Accuracy

A real-time backtest on the synthetic world: Model 2 run at each as-of date with only what was published by then (re-estimating each time), its sector totals scored against the truth. Error is the absolute log error of the member median (absolute difference for utilization); coverage is the share of truths inside the 90% member band. The backtest runs use unwidened bands.
