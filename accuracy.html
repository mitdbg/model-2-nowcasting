Accuracy

The backtest runs Model 2 at each as-of date on the synthetic world, using only observations published by then and re-estimating each time, and scores every sector total against the truth. The backtest runs use unwidened bands.

  • Error: absolute log error of the member median (absolute difference for utilization and load factor; none where the truth or the median is zero).
  • Coverage: share of months with the truth inside the 90% band.

Parameter recovery

Each row compares fitted with true parameters of the tracked operations in this run's fit, in prior sds.

  • Bias: mean of fit minus truth.
  • RMSE: root mean square of fit minus truth.
  • Correlation: correlation of fit and truth across operations, which shows whether the fit recovers differences between operations.
