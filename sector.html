Tracked operations and representative entities

Each sector total above is the sum of two parts. Tracked operations are specific plants, airline networks and data-center campuses that the model follows one by one, each with its own reports. Representative entities stand in for every other establishment in the sector: one per size class and region, scaled to the establishment counts in Census County Business Patterns, and informed mostly by Census employment. Dashed lines are each part's truth. The bands are the model's own 90% bands, not widened like the totals above, so they are too narrow (see Accuracy).
