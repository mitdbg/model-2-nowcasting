Sector totals

Each chart shows the median and widened 90% band of a sector total, with the truth (dashed). Shading marks nowcast months (after the last observation) and forecast months (after the as-of date). The ⓘ button defines each quantity.

Tracked and representative entities

Each sector total above is the sum of two parts. Tracked entities are specific mills, airline networks and data-center campuses that the model follows one by one, each with its own observations. Representative entities stand in for every other establishment in the sector: one per size class and region, scaled to the establishment counts in Census County Business Patterns and informed mostly by Census employment. Dashed lines show each part's truth. The bands here are unwidened, so they are too narrow (see Accuracy).

Check against Model 1

Model 1 nowcasts an official series for the sector, and Model 2 uses that nowcast as a pseudo-observation of the sector total (L16). Each chart compares the two in the series' units.

Information from the data

Each row compares a sector total's variance after the observations with its variance from the model's dynamics alone (posterior over prior variance, across history). Near 1, the data add little, and the run flags the total as prior-dominated.
