Firms behind the sector totals

Model 2 estimates monthly quantities for individual operations (steel mills, airline networks, data-center campuses): capacity, output, order books and prices. It learns from scattered observations: company filings, emissions, satellite readings and traffic statistics. Representative operations stand in for establishments without their own data, one per sector, size class and region. Model 2 sums the operations to sector totals and checks the totals against Model 1's sector nowcasts.

Known problems

  • All sectors: 90% bands miss the truth too often. Unwidened, they hold it 66% of the time across all sector quantities (52% for capacity, output, utilization, order book and shipments). Sector totals use widened bands (PLAN decision 17); operation charts use unwidened bands.
  • Steel: the order book runs about 8% high at the nowcast.
  • Steel: finished inventory runs 4–5 times the truth (in band 1% of months). No observation covers it, so the prior sets it.
  • Steel and data centers: lead time runs about 12% low (data centers: in band 0% of months). Lead-time observations carry a per-source bias that can absorb the error.
  • Data centers: normal occupancy fits low (fitted 0.74, true 0.86). No observation covers it directly.
  • Data centers: site order books run well above the truth, although company order-book observations fit. The model splits each company's order book across its sites wrongly.
