Evidence: observations and their sources

Everything the model learns from outside data. Entity observations (§6.6) inform the hidden states of the operations in their scope, and through estimation their parameters. Model 1's sector series enter as reconciliation (see each sector page). The full written inventory is OBSERVATION_SOURCES.md.

Observation types

Counts are observations published by the as-of date over the whole synthetic history; "in window" are those attached to this run. Lag is the median time from the end of the period covered to publication. Fit: the share of observations inside the model's 90% predictive band (should be near 90%; well above means σ is set too large), and the variance of the filter's standardized innovations (should be near 1; above 1 means the noise σ is set too small or the model is biased for that type).
