Possible Improvements

Each fix is labeled by its problem's number and a letter (1A, 1B, 2A, ...); below the label is the start of its lasting id, and below each problem the start of the problem's (point at one for the whole id). Lasting ids never change and are unique across all worlds and forecasts. Sort by clicking a blue column heading: by problem (the fixes for each problem together), by likelihood of success (most likely first), by the time a test would take, or by what it would cost.
